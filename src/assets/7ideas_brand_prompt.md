
# 7iDeas — AI Brand Prompt System

This document defines the visual, conceptual and communication rules for generating design assets for the brand **7iDeas**.

All generated designs must respect these brand constraints.

---

# Brand Identity

**Brand name:** 7iDeas

**Category:**  
Argentinian software services company.

**Description**

7iDeas is an Argentinian technology company specialized in:

- custom software development  
- system integration  
- technological strategy for medium-sized companies  

The company acts as a **technological partner**, not just a software provider.

The focus is **business technology management**, not just coding.

---

# Brand Positioning

7iDeas positions itself as:

- a technological ally
- a strategic partner
- a business-oriented technology company

Key concept:

**Technology with criteria.  
Technology with responsibility.  
Technology integrated into business.**

---

# Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

- professional
- reliable
- strategic
- intelligent
- modern
- structured
- minimal
- corporate

Avoid:

- playful startup aesthetics
- excessive colors
- chaotic layouts
- overly futuristic cyberpunk style

The style should feel **corporate technology consulting**.

# Visual Style

## Layout

- minimal composition
- strong visual hierarchy
- large whitespace
- structured grid
- corporate aesthetic

## Graphic Language

Use:

- geometric shapes
- squares
- rectangles
- frames
- diagonal lines
- layered compositions

Avoid:

- organic shapes
- illustrative cartoons
- decorative graphics

- black and dark gray dominate layouts
- white space is important
- yellow used only as highlight

The design system can include:

- squares
- circles
- plus symbol (+)

- transparent brand symbol overlays

- direct

# Output Instructions for AI

When generating assets for 7iDeas:

1. Use the brand color palette.
2. Maintain minimal corporate layouts.
3. Use geometric composition.
4. Prioritize clarity and structure.
5. Avoid decorative or playful design.

The result should feel like **a high-level technology consulting firm**.
